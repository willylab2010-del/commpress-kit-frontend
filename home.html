How it works

1

Drop your file

Drag and drop or click to pick files from your device.

2

Choose settings

Pick quality, format, size or compression level.

3

Hit Compress

Processing runs entirely inside your browser — no server.

4

Download

Save the compressed file straight to your device.

🔐

Your files stay on your device

All compression runs inside your browser using JavaScript, the Canvas API, and WebCodecs. No file is ever sent to a server. No account, no tracking, no size limits imposed by a backend — just your CPU doing the work locally.
